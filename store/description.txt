Inkfold turns Chrome into a fast, private PDF annotator. Open any PDF and start marking it up: no account, no uploads, nothing to set up.

HIGHLIGHT, DRAW AND WRITE
• A highlighter that follows the lines of real text, in any colour
• Rectangles and ellipses to call out figures, tables and key passages
• Text boxes anywhere on the page, in any size and colour, with LaTeX math: type $E = mc^2$ and it's typeset
• Move, resize, recolour or delete anything you've added, and undo or redo every step

TURN MARKDOWN INTO NOTE PAGES
Press N and type a note, or use Markdown you copied from your notes app, an AI chat or anywhere else. Inkfold adds a cleanly typeset note page right after the page you're reading, as wide as that page and exactly as tall as your note. Headings, lists, checklists, tables, code, quotes and links are all supported, and so is LaTeX math ($inline$, $$display$$ and align-style environments), drawn as sharp vector graphics.

ORGANISE PAGES
• Right-click any page to rotate or delete it, or work on ranges like 2-4, 7, 10-end
• Split a PDF into several files in one step: 1-3, 4-8, 9-end
• Everything can be undone

READ COMFORTABLY
• Fit width, fit page and two-page view, smooth even on long documents
• See which page you're on, and jump to any page by number
• Selectable, searchable text that works with Ctrl+F and screen readers
• PDFs stay in their own tab with their normal address, so AI assistants built into your browser, such as Gemini in Chrome, can still read them

KEYBOARD FIRST
Every action has a shortcut, shown in brackets on its button. Press ? to see them all.

WORKS EVERYWHERE YOUR PDF GOES
Your highlights, shapes and notes are saved as standard PDF annotations, so they show up in Acrobat, Preview, Chrome's own viewer and other apps, and stay editable when you reopen the file in Inkfold. Saving always creates a new copy; your original file is never changed.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Inkfold runs entirely in your browser. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and your documents are never uploaded. It's free and open source: https://github.com/MeetCoding/inkfold

GOOD TO KNOW
• PDFs you open in Chrome open in Inkfold. To use Chrome's own viewer instead, right-click the Inkfold icon and untick "Open every PDF with Inkfold".
• For PDFs on your computer, turn on "Allow access to file URLs" in Inkfold's details page.
